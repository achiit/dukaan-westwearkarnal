About

A small store with a simple idea.

We started West & Wear two years ago because we were tired of choosing between clothes that look good and clothes that feel good. Our first rack was small, and my cousin helped me fold every shirt before opening day.

Today we stock everyday fashion for men and women: plain tees, relaxed jeans, hoodies for Haryana winters and a streetwear corner for the younger crowd. Nothing loud, nothing that goes out of style by next month.

Come in, try things on, take your time. If you can't visit, message us on WhatsApp and we will send photos.

  • Comfort first: soft fabrics, honest fits
  • Simple designs that go with what you already own
  • Men's and women's ranges under one roof
  • Photos and size help on WhatsApp
  • New seasonal pieces every few weeks
